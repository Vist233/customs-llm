## The builds are not signed

- **macOS:** the app is not signed or notarized, so Gatekeeper blocks it. After dragging it to Applications, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/customs-llm.app`, or right-click it and choose Open.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

## Known limits

- Tested in Docker against LiteLLM 1.108.0, magpie 0.1.3915 and opencode 1.19.5, and with opencode against the real opencode Go service. new-api, Portkey and claude-code-router have not been run.
- A FAIL means the behaviour in the issue appears. It is not always a bug; some are design choices.
- The fake upstream's replies are mostly rebuilt from issue text, so streamed results depend on how accurate they are.
- The command-line text and the agent handbook are in Chinese for now. The web page is in English with a Chinese switch.
